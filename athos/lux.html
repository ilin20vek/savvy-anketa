The Holy Mountain · Garden of the Mother of God

Where the earth touches heaven

A pilgrimage to Mount Athos - a thousand years of unbroken prayer, twenty monasteries, and a dawn above the clouds you carry for the rest of your life.

Scroll
❧

For a thousand years the lamps have not gone out, and the prayer has never fallen silent. Men come here not for the sights, but for a stillness found nowhere in the world.

The Holy Mountain

An autonomous monastic republic, reached only by sea

No roads lead in. A single ferry carries pilgrims past monasteries that rise straight from the rock - and leaves the world behind.

How the journey unfolds

The Pilgrimage

I

Permit & dates

We arrange your diamonitirion - the personal permit of the Holy Community - and choose dates that open the right services and the right elders to you.

II

The passage

Flight, Thessaloniki, Ouranoupoli, the ferry. You are met and accompanied - not by an agent, but by someone who knows the Mountain and returns to it often.

III

Monasteries & vigils

Lodging within the monasteries, meals with the brotherhood, night-long vigils, veneration of the relics. Between the houses - on foot along mountain paths, or by jeep.

IV

The return

You carry home not photographs but silence. We stay in touch - for a blessing, a word, or the next road to the Mountain.

The Summit

Dawn above the clouds, at two thousand metres

The climb to the peak of Athos ends at the small chapel of the Transfiguration - clouds underfoot, and a sun that rises as if for you alone.
