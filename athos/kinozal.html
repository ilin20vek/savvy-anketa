A mountain you go to
not for the views

Athos is a monastic republic under the protection of the Mother of God - a thousand years of unbroken prayer. We do not carry tourists. We prepare the pilgrim: the diamonitirion, nights in the monasteries, meetings with the elders, the service at dawn.

"Here time moves differently. For over a decade we have led people to this place - and each one returns a different man."
