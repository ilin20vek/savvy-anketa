Three design directions for the pilgrimage website. Same material, three different voices - from quiet and noble to cinematic and commercial. Open each in full and choose the one that feels right.

Concept 01
Restrained · Noble

The Pilgrim's Path

A long, story-driven page: parchment and darkness alternating, the climb to the summit told step by step, a quiet gallery and a form at the end. For those who feel Athos as silence, not a tour.
